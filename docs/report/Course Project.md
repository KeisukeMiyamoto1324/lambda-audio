## Overview

The purpose of the course project is to apply what have been studying in this course to solve a problem of your interest.

## Problem Selection

Select and conduct research on a topic in the area of machine learning. Suggested topics, some are discussed in the course content:

- Extension of basic autoencoders. VAE, contractive, feature disentangling, etc.
- Deep learning–based image watermarking. Multibit and robust scenario.
- Explainable Deep learning. To explain a model without an input.
- Attention Module. Spatial/Channel/Other domains
- Object Detection. Region proposal or regression methods.
- Multitask and incremental learning. Knowledge retention and transfer.
- Robustness of a deep learning model. Attacks, proactive/passive defense.
- Anomaly detection. With/without class label provided.
- Generative models. Auto regressor, different advanced GANs, etc.
- Human action/activity detection. Skeleton-based, two stream (RGB and Optical flow), 3D CNN and LSTM, etc.

Please email the instructor for approval if you would like to select a topic outside the suggestions.

*For students who were in my class CSCI 8590 (Fundamentals of Deep Learning)*

Any reused submissions will NOT earn any points. It is suggested to select a different topic from our previous course. However, if you want to select the same topic, you must present and highlight at least 50% new contents (newly implemented or proposed methods).

## Project Types

The project can be of three main types.

1. **Application project (baseline).** This is anticipated to be the most common. It is an application that interests a student, and the student will explore how best to apply deep learning algorithms to solve it.
2. **Algorithmic project.** The student will pick a problem or family of problems, and develop a new or a variant of an existing algorithm, to solve it.
3. **Theoretical project.** The students will develop, prove, and propose novel and non-trivial properties of a deep learning algorithm. This is often quite difficult and anticipated to be rare.

## Submissions

1. **Write a report paper** containing at least the following sections:
   1. Introduction (What is your topic in this paper? What are the problems to be solved? What can be the applications if your discussed solutions are successful?)
   2. Related work (What is the current status in the literature? What methods and ideas did the related papers propose? What are the advantages of your selected (or proposed) method?)
   3. Methodology (Discuss in detail your implemented (or proposed) deep learning model, figures and equations can be helpful in the illustration)
   4. Experiment (What are the datasets applied? What are the experiments conducted, to show the features or advantages of your implemented (or proposed) deep learning model? What are the results? Figures and tables can be helpful in the illustration)
   5. Conclusion (Summarize the topic, methodology, and advantages)

2. **Runnable Python (Keras or Pytorch) codes** associated to support the results presented in your paper.
